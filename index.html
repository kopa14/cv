Charlemagne Chocolatiers · Oct 2022

Economic Mission — Birmingham, United Kingdom

  • 1 weekon the ground in Birmingham
  • 2 shopsindependent organic stores
  • 10,000branded chocolates ordered by The Grand Hotel

Through a collaboration between AWEX and HEPL, I had the opportunity to take part in a real economic mission designed to help Walloon companies explore and develop opportunities in foreign markets, while giving students hands-on experience in international business and export.

Participation was selective. To qualify, I first conducted a market analysis of Wallonia and the UK, focusing on either the agri-food or pharmaceutical sector. I then developed a complete export strategy based on a simulated Walloon company entering the UK market. The project was presented to a panel of AWEX representatives and HEPL professors, who selected the students they felt were best prepared to represent a Walloon company abroad.

After being selected, I had to find a company to represent: together with my team, we reached an agreement with Charlemagne Chocolatiers. With my team's help, I then developed a prospecting strategy combining desk research, cold calling and cold emailing to identify potential British customers and secure meetings ahead of the mission.

During my week in Birmingham, I met with potential clients, visited businesses I had identified as relevant prospects and presented Charlemagne Chocolatiers at the Birmingham Chamber of Commerce. The mission resulted in sales to two independent organic shops and, more importantly, an order of 10,000 branded chocolates negotiated with The Grand Hotel Birmingham, with the contract then finalized by Charlemagne Chocolatiers' sales manager. The agreement also included the possibility of recurring monthly orders of a further 10,000 units if the initial launch was successful.

The experience gave me first-hand exposure to international prospecting, B2B sales, client meetings, commercial pitching and export business development, from market research all the way through to negotiating real commercial opportunities.
